About / Bio

Background

Based in Gainesville, Florida, I work at the intersection of signals & systems, controls, machine learning, and neuroengineering at the University of Florida. I care about mathematically grounded models that stay useful when deployed in real pipelines with real latency, noise, and hardware constraints.

Before graduate school, I attended Doral Academy Charter Middle/High School, where I was active in Mu Alpha Theta, Zeta Omicron, Robotics Club, and Physics-Chemistry club. Those years shaped my interest in math-heavy problem solving, competition, and hands-on engineering.

Outside research, I spend time on neuroscience, powerbuilding, music, and photography around Gainesville. Music has been part of my life for as long as I can remember, and I still occasionally DJ and produce. I value the same discipline outside the lab that I expect inside it.
