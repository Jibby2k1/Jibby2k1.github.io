Research and projects

From Gainesville, Florida, my work sits in two umbrellas: (1) research in the University of Florida's Computational NeuroEngineering Lab (CNEL) and (2) engineering and community projects through the IEEE Signal Processing Society (SPS) student chapter at UF. The recurring themes are signal processing, time-series machine learning, neuroengineering, and reproducible research systems.
