Research overview

Research, publications, and public proof

My current research builds machine-learning surrogates for physics simulation in the SmartDATA Lab, alongside neuroengineering work in CNEL and engineering, workshop, and prototype work through IEEE SPS at UF. The common thread is modeling that remains useful when the data is noisy, the physics is unforgiving, and the deployment constraints are real.

CNEL · sequence modeling · persistent memory

Kernel Adaptive Memory: testing persistent kernel memory for sequence models

Six phases of preregistered experiments asking whether a kernel-routed persistent memory helps sequence models: no general advantage yet, but the hypothesis has narrowed from dense radial attention to sparse, top-K-routed separable memory.

CNEL · dynamical systems · classification

Latent-signal classification with hierarchical dynamical systems

Pipeline that maps noisy multichannel time-series observations to compact latent trajectories with a Hierarchical Linear Dynamical System (HLDS), then evaluates those trajectories for classification, separability, and stability.
