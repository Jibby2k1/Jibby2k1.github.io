From the lessons to the repository#

Every lesson builds a small version of something the Avatar repository does on real footage. This page lists, lesson by lesson, the files that do the same job there, and the main way in which the real code differs from the toy. Paths are relative to the repository root and link to the main branch.

Three differences hold for every row and are not repeated:

  • the real pipeline runs on a GPU, on captured video of a person, with licensed body models (SMPL, MANO, FLAME) that the repository does not contain;

  • poses, masks and keypoints come from off-the-shelf trackers and are imperfect, where the toy knows them;

  • the base model and its rasteriser are upstream Gaussian Shell Maps [Abdal et al., 2024] and 3D Gaussian splatting [Kerbl et al., 2023], cloned separately. The repository holds the fitting, tracking, evaluation and speed work around them.

Part 2 · Gaussian splatting#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

2a · 2D Gaussians and compositing

the upstream rasteriser (not in the repository); modified copies in ext/dgr_warp/ and ext/dgr_label/

A tile-based CUDA rasteriser instead of a PyTorch loop over bounding boxes; the copies add a warp and a label output

2b · From 3D to the image

the same rasteriser; gsmfit/core.py calls it

Cameras come from a calibration or from an assumed field of view, not from a known toy camera

2c · Gradients

ext/adam_exact/, ext/ssim_exact/

Exact, fused Adam and SSIM kernels in CUDA; the equivalence evidence for each speed-up is in (docs/speed.md)

Part 3 · Body models and skinning#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

3a · Skeleton and pose

gsmfit/articulation.py, gsmfit/posefit.py, fit/fit_shape_multiview.py

The SMPL skeleton and a fitted body shape instead of 17 hand-placed joints; poses start from a tracker

3b · Skinning

gsmfit/articulation.py, gsmfit/webbing.py, docs/webbing.md

SMPL’s learned skinning weights; a fix for the shreds that appear between the thighs when a dance spreads the legs wider than the capture did

Part 4 · Gaussian Shell Maps#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

4a · Shell maps

gsmfit/shells.py, gsmfit/normal_offset.py

Shells around the SMPL mesh with one shared UV texture; a texture channel holds a learned offset along the normal

4b · Sampling and density

gsmfit/face_density.py, gsmfit/foot_density.py, gsmfit/hand_hifi.py

One weighted sampler shared by the face, hand and foot density options

Part 5 · Fitting from video#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

5a · Fit from video

fit/fit_multi.py, tools/inpaint_unobserved.py, pipeline/build_sam2_data.py

One shared texture fitted to all training frames for 30,000 iterations, with SAM 2 masks; surfaces that no frame saw are inpainted afterwards

5b · When frames disagree

fit/refine_pose.py, fit/refine_rigid.py, fit/track_partitions.py

Per-frame articulated pose correction from masks and keypoints, never from colour, followed by a refit; each split is tracked on its own

6a · Crops and parts

gsmfit/hifi_preset.py, gsmfit/hifi_crop.py, gsmfit/hand_rig.py, gsmfit/flame_rig.py, gsmfit/mouth_proxy.py, gsmfit/closeup_place.py, docs/hifi.md

Part 7 · Motion and contact#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

7a · Contact and leg IK

gsmfit/leg_ik.py, docs/leg_ik.md, eval/feet_eval.py

Two-bone IK on planted feet, opt-in in eval/perform.py; it cut the world-space foot slide while the on-screen slide did not change

7b · Flight and metrics

eval/perform.py, gsmfit/leg_ik.py, docs/research_brief.md

The centre-of-mass flight signal and the vector angular acceleration are opt-in fixes from an audit

7c · Secondary motion

gsmfit/secmotion.py, docs/plus-secmotion.md, docs/mobile_parts.md

Built and off by default; memoryless today, so the displacement returns to zero when motion stops

Part 8 · Evaluation#

Lesson

Real files

What differs

8a · Splits, seeds, metrics

eval/evaluate.py, gsmfit/evaluation_contract.py, gsmfit/split_identity.py, eval/hifi/region_metrics.py, prep/p118v3/sealed/score_sealed.sh, planning/EVALUATION_CONTRACTS.md

9 · Speed

docs/speed.md, tools/speedv2/, bench/, gsmfit/speedv2.py, gsmfit/fasttrain.py

CUDA kernels and equivalence tiers: bit-identical paths on by default, reordered floating point only where it stays inside run-to-run noise, reduced precision rejected

Where results live#

  • RESULTS.md: the dated record of every measured result, including the sealed evaluation.

  • docs/results_tour.md: held-out close-ups, ablations and the standard track.

  • docs/HISTORY.md and docs/RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md: how the project got here and where it is going.

  • tests/: unit, identity and integration tests; the CPU tests run anywhere.
