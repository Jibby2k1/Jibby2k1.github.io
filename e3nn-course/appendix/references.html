References

References#

Works cited across the course. Each lesson also lists its own references at the end. The full bibliography lives in course/references.bib.

The e3nn foundations come from the MolSSI e3nn course. The physics of suspensions and Stokes flow is covered by [Happel and Brenner, 1983] and [Guazzelli and Morris, 2011]. Attention and set models are covered by [Vaswani et al., 2017], [Zaheer et al., 2017] and [Lee et al., 2019]. Equivariant message passing is covered by [Satorras et al., 2021] and [Batatia et al., 2022]. Anderson acceleration is covered by [Walker and Ni, 2011], and Lebedev quadrature by [Lebedev and Laikov, 1999].
