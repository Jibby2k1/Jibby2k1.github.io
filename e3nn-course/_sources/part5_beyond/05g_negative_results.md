## What you will learn

- reproduce on the toy three instructive failures: a non-equivariant transformer, pair-geometry removed, refinement on top of a strong model
- learn to read a negative result: what it rules out and what it does not

## Outline

Uses the toy to show results that did not work as hoped (qualitatively, as in the research): a raw-coordinate transformer that is only approximately equivariant even with augmentation, attention without pair geometry, and what refinement adds once the model already captures interactions. Each is framed as a hypothesis, a test and a conclusion.
