# References

Works cited across the course. Each lesson also lists its own references at the end. The full bibliography lives
in `course/references.bib`.

The e3nn foundations come from the [MolSSI e3nn course](https://molssi-ai.github.io/e3nn-course/book/intro.html).
The physics of suspensions and Stokes flow is covered by {cite}`happel1983low` and {cite}`guazzelli2011physical`.
Attention and set models are covered by {cite}`vaswani2017attention`, {cite}`zaheer2017deepsets` and
{cite}`lee2019settransformer`. Equivariant message passing is covered by {cite}`satorras2021egnn` and
{cite}`batatia2022mace`. Anderson acceleration is covered by {cite}`walker2011anderson`, and Lebedev quadrature
by {cite}`lebedev1999quadrature`.
