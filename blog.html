Field Notes

Notes, write-ups, and lab logs

Short entries filed as an ongoing notebook. The goal is quick retrieval and readable context, not feed-style churn.

How entries are filed

Drop blog images into assets/img/blog/, then add or duplicate a post entry in data/blog.json. The page renders each entry as a card automatically.

Metadata

Use title, date, tag, img, imgAlt, summary, and content. Optional imgFit and imgAspect let you tune each entry without changing the renderer.
