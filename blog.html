Blog

Notes, write-ups, and logs

Short notes, project logs, and technical reflections. Posts are laid out as cards so the page stays easier to browse.

Easy to update

Drop blog images into assets/img/blog/, then add or duplicate a post entry in data/blog.json. The page now renders each post as a card automatically.

Suggested post fields

Use title, date, tag, img, summary, and content. If you want, I can also add links, reading time, or categories next.
